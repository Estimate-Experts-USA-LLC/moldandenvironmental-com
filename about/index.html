About Mold and Environmental

Mold and Environmental is a Florida-licensed mold assessment and mold remediation company serving the Treasure Coast. We perform inspections and sampling, or containment and removal - quoted as separate engagements, in line with Florida licensing rules.

In addition to mold services, our company also offers water damage restoration. So if you’ve been affected by a flood or other water-related issue, please don’t hesitate to call us. Our team of experienced professionals is here to help you every step of the way. For more information on our services or to schedule an appointment, please give us a call today!
