Local conditions

What we see in West Palm Beach

Condominium and multi-unit buildings

A great deal of West Palm housing is multi-unit. That changes the job: moisture can originate in a neighbouring unit or a shared riser, and access and permissions have to be arranged with the association as well as the owner.

Shared systems, shared problems

Where corridors, risers and AC systems are common to a building, a problem in one unit is worth checking against the units around it. Treating one apartment in isolation sometimes misses the source entirely.

Commercial and mixed-use

Offices, retail units and mixed-use buildings have their own moisture patterns -- large AC plant, long duct runs and spaces that sit empty out of hours.

Common questions

West Palm Beach questions

Do you work in condos and apartment buildings?

Yes. Be aware that access may need agreement from the association or the building manager as well as from you.

The damp seems to come from next door. What then?

That is common in multi-unit buildings. We report what we find and where the moisture appears to originate; who is responsible for fixing it is a matter for the building and the owners.

Do you cover commercial property?

Yes, residential and commercial. Tell us the type of building and roughly the area involved when you call.
