How to check them

Go to MyFloridaLicense.com, choose to search by licence number, and enter MRSA3298 or MRSR3880. The register shows the holder's name, the status and the expiry date, straight from the Department of Business and Professional Regulation.

We would rather you did that than took a photograph of a certificate at face value. A printed document shows what was true on the day it was issued; the register shows what is true today.

An important detail

Florida licenses people, not companies

For mold assessment and mold remediation, the Department of Business and Professional Regulation issues licences to individuals. There is no such thing as a DBPR mold business licence, so any company describing itself that way is describing something that does not exist.

Our work is performed under the two licences above, both held by Mauricio Gutierrez. We say it that way because it is the accurate way to say it.

Why it matters to you

Two licences, one role per project

Florida separates assessment from remediation deliberately: the party deciding how much work is needed should not be the party paid to carry it out. We hold both licences, and on any single project we take one role only.

If we assess your property, an independent remediator does the removal. If we do the removal, an independent assessor verifies it. We will tell you which applies before anything is quoted.

Common questions

Licence questions

Are you certified as well as licensed?

We list only what we can evidence, and what we can evidence is the two Florida DBPR licences above. If we hold anything else worth stating, it will appear here with a number you can check — not as a logo.

Why do you not show certificates on the site?

Because a certificate image shows a moment in the past, and a licence register shows the present. Two of the documents previously displayed on this site had in fact expired. The register is the better answer for you.

Is the company insured?

We are not making an insurance claim on this website. If insurance status matters to your project, ask us directly and we will give you a straight answer in writing.

Can you work outside Florida?

These licences are issued by the State of Florida and apply in Florida.
