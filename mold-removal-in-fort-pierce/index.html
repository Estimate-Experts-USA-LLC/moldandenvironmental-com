Local conditions

What we see in Fort Pierce

This is our home base

Our registered address is 4206 Troon Pl, Fort Pierce, FL 34947. Fort Pierce jobs involve the least travel of anywhere we work, which usually means more flexibility on timing.

Older housing stock near the water

Much of the older Fort Pierce housing near the Indian River Lagoon and the inlet was built before modern vapour barriers and sealing practices. Wind-driven rain and long humid spells find their way into wall assemblies that were never detailed to shed it.

Storm and surge exposure

Properties east of US-1 and along the waterfront take the brunt of storm season. What matters for mold is not the storm itself but whether materials that got wet were dried within a couple of days.
