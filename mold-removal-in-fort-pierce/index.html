How does a mold allergy manifest itself?

An airborne spore produced by mold is similar to pollen. Musty odour, visible growth, or recent water intrusion are the usual reasons to book an assessment. Upon inhaling spores, your body believes they will harm you and attacks them, resulting in many classic allergy symptoms. Sneezing, itchy skin, rashes, hives, itching nose, sore throat, coughing, trouble sleeping, and trouble breathing may be symptoms of this reaction. Health questions are for your doctor. If you can see or smell mold, or have had water intrusion, call us and we will tell you whether an assessment is warranted.
