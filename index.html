Which one do you need?

Not sure whether you need testing or removal?

They are two different jobs, and in Florida they are two different licenses. Most people start with an assessment, because until you know where the moisture is coming from you do not yet know what removal would involve.

Assessment — inspect the property, investigate the moisture, sample where it is appropriate, and document what was found.

Remediation — contain the area, remove affected material, clean, control the moisture source, and record the work.

On any given project we perform one of the two roles, not both. If you need the other, we will tell you plainly and you can bring in an independent provider.

Common questions

Before you call

Do I need mold testing?

Not always. If mold is already visible and the cause is obvious, testing may add little. Testing helps when you cannot see the source, when you need documentation, or when you need to know what is in the air rather than on a surface.

What happens during a mold inspection?

We walk the property, look at the areas where moisture collects, take moisture readings, and check the places mold is most likely to be found. We tell you what we examined and what we did not.

How long does it take?

Most residential inspections take a couple of hours on site. If samples go to a laboratory, results normally follow within a few working days.

Do you do the removal as well?

We hold both Florida licenses, but on any single project we perform one role, not both. If we assessed your property, an independent remediator does the removal — and the other way round.

Can mold come back after remediation?

Yes, if the moisture source is still there. Removal deals with the material; it does not fix a leaking roof or a plumbing problem. We tell you what needs correcting and whether it needs another trade.

How do I check your licenses?

Search MRSA3298 or MRSR3880 at MyFloridaLicense.com. Both are held by Mauricio Gutierrez and are current.
