Local conditions

What we see in Fort Lauderdale

Waterfront living, on and off the water

Fort Lauderdale has more waterfront property and more boats than anywhere else we cover. We inspect both -- houses on the canals and the vessels moored behind them.

Vessels and marine mold

A boat is a sealed, humid box sitting on water. If your concern is a vessel rather than a building, our yacht inspection page covers how that work differs.

Dense, mature housing stock

Older Broward housing frequently carries decades of additions, re-roofs and AC replacements. Moisture tends to appear where one era of construction meets another.

What we can do here

Services available in Fort Lauderdale

Assessment — MRSA3298

Inspection, moisture investigation, sampling where it is warranted, and a written report with a remediation protocol if one is needed.

Mold Inspection & Testing

Remediation — MRSR3880

Containment, removal of affected material, cleaning and drying, worked to a protocol and independently verified afterwards.

Mold Removal & Remediation

One role per project

We hold both Florida licences but take only one role on any single project, so the company deciding how much work is needed is never the company paid to do it. In Fort Lauderdale as everywhere else, we will tell you which applies before anything is quoted.
