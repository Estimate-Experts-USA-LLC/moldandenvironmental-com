Mold Inspection & Testing

We inspect the property, find where the moisture is coming from, sample where it will tell you something useful, and give you a written report you can act on.

When it is worth calling

Reasons people ask for an inspection

You do not need all of these. Any one of them is a reasonable reason to have someone look.

A persistent musty smell

Especially one that comes back after cleaning, or is stronger in one room.

A leak, storm or flood

Water that reached building materials and may not have dried properly.

Visible growth

On walls, ceilings, around windows, under sinks or behind furniture.

Staining or bubbling

Discoloured drywall, lifting paint or warped trim — usually a moisture sign.

Buying or selling

Documentation for a transaction, or a condition raised during a survey.

After remediation

Independent verification that removal work was completed properly.

On site

What we actually look at

A visual assessment of the areas where moisture collects and mold is most likely to be found — around plumbing, under sinks, behind appliances, at window and door reveals, in bathrooms, attics, crawl spaces and around HVAC.

We also record what we could not examine. Wall cavities, sealed voids and areas behind fixed furniture cannot be inspected without opening them up, and a report that does not say so is not telling you the whole picture.

The part that matters most

Moisture investigation

Mold is a moisture problem. Finding growth tells you where it is; finding the water tells you why. We use moisture meters on suspect materials and thermal imaging to find temperature differences that can indicate damp behind a surface.

Thermal imaging does not see mold, and it does not see through walls. It shows temperature difference, which is a clue about where water may be — a starting point for a meter reading, not a conclusion on its own.

Sampling

Testing is not automatic

Sampling costs money and takes time, so it should earn its place. Sometimes it does, and sometimes it does not — we will tell you which.

When sampling helps

When you cannot see the source, when you need documentation for a transaction or a dispute, when you want a comparison against outdoor air, or when you need to verify an area after remediation.

When it may not

When growth is already visible and the cause is obvious, a sample often confirms what you can see. In that situation the money is usually better spent on fixing the moisture.

Where we do sample, the samples are analysed by an independent third-party laboratory — not by us. Laboratory results describe what was present in the samples that were taken, at the time they were taken. They are not a complete inventory of everything in a building, and no sampling method can be.

The deliverable

What you receive

A written assessment

What was inspected, what was found, what was not examined, and the moisture readings that support it.

Laboratory results

Where samples were taken, the independent laboratory's analysis, explained in plain language rather than left as a table.

A remediation protocol

Where remediation is warranted, a written scope you can hand to a remediation contractor to quote against.

Afterwards

What happens next

01

You get the report

Read it, ask us anything that is unclear. There is no obligation attached to it.

02

Fix the moisture

If a roof, plumbing or drainage problem caused it, that trade needs to correct it or the mold returns.

03

Remediation, if needed

The protocol goes to a remediation contractor. If we assessed the property, that is a different company.

04

Verification

Where wanted, an independent assessment after the work confirms the area was properly cleared.

Why we will not do both on your project

In Florida, mold assessment and mold remediation are separate licences, and the person deciding what work is needed should not be the person paid to carry it out. We hold both licences, but on any one project we perform one role only. It is a protection for you, not a limitation on you.

Common questions

Mold inspection questions

How long does an inspection take?

Most residential inspections take a couple of hours on site, depending on the size of the property and how many areas need checking. If samples are taken, laboratory results normally follow within a few working days.

Do I have to have samples taken?

No. Sampling is a decision, not a default. If visible growth already explains the problem, we will say that testing may add little.

Will testing tell me every type of mold in my home?

No, and any company that says otherwise is overstating it. A sample describes what was in that sample at that moment. It is evidence, not a complete inventory of a building.

Can you tell me if the mold is making someone ill?

No. We assess buildings, not people. Health questions belong with a doctor. What we can tell you is what is present in the property and where the moisture is coming from.

Do you remove the mold as well?

We hold the remediation licence, but not on a project we have assessed. You would use an independent remediation contractor, working to the written protocol we provide.
