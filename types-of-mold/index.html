Types of Mold

Posted on April 12, 2023 by admin

WHAT IS MOLD?

Mold is a type of fungus that grows in moist and warm environments and propagates through air. It is harmful to your property and health. According to some resources, there are more than 100000 types of mold, and they can vary in color, texture, and toxicity.

Types of Mold:

This article will discuss some of the most common types of mold.

Aspergillus

Aspergillus is one of the most common types of mold that can be found indoors and outdoors. It can grow on food, in soil, and in building materials. Aspergillus can cause several allergies and respiratory problems, leading to other health issues.

Cladosporium

Cladosporium is a type of mold that can be found in both indoor and outdoor environments. It typically grows on damp surfaces like walls, ceilings, and carpets. It can cause allergies, asthma, and other respiratory health issues.

Penicillium

Penicillium is a mold commonly found in indoor environments, such as homes, offices, and schools. It can grow on food, wallpaper, and insulation. Some types of Penicillium are used to make antibiotics, while others can cause allergies and other health problems.

Stachybotrys

Stachybotrys, also known as “black mold,” is a type of mold that is often associated with excessive water damage. It can grow on wet materials and places, such as wood, paper, and drywall. Stachybotrys is associated with prolonged water damage and typically indicates a long-running moisture source.

Fusarium

Fusarium is a type of mold that can be found in soil and on plants. It can also grow indoors, particularly in damp or humid environments. Fusarium can cause respiratory problems, eye infections, and other health issues.

Alternaria

Alternaria is a mold mostly found outdoors, but it can also grow indoors if preventive measures are not taken. It typically grows on damp surfaces like wallpaper, carpets, and window frames. Alternaria can cause allergies, asthma, and other respiratory problems.

Chaetomium

Chaetomium is a type of mold that is often found in water-damaged buildings. It can grow on materials that are rich in cellulose, such as drywall, wallpaper, and carpets. Chaetomium can cause allergies, respiratory problems, and several health issues if not treated on time.

BEST WAYS TO GET RID OF MOLD

When it comes to dealing with mold, there are two main options: mold removal and mold remediation and sanitization. Although these terms are sometimes used interchangeably, both are different processes.

Mold Removal

Mold removal is a process of removing actual mold from the affected structure and material. This is often the first step in dealing with a mold problem. Mold removal typically involves physically removing the mold from surfaces using a variety of methods, including scrubbing, bleaching, or removing it with specialized equipment. Mold removal aims to eliminate visible mold growth and prevent further spread.

While mold removal is an important part of dealing with a mold problem, it is not always sufficient on its own. This is because mold spores can still be present in the air, even after removing visible mold. Most of the time, mold remediation is necessary to eliminate mold permanently from your property.

Mold Remediation:

Mold remediation is a more comprehensive approach to dealing with mold infestation. Remediation involves removing visible mold, addressing the underlying cause of mold growth, and taking steps to prevent future growth. This may involve identifying and repairing sources of moisture, improving ventilation, installing dehumidifiers or air purifiers, and getting rid of the material or furniture that has been excessively damaged by mold.

Mold remediation process:

Assessment: 

A thorough inspection of the property is conducted to identify the extent of the mold propagation and the underlying cause of the mold growth.

Containment: 

Measures are taken to contain the mold and prevent it from spreading during remediation. This may involve sealing off affected areas or using negative air pressure to prevent mold spores from spreading to other areas of the building.

Remediation:

In this process, experts remove mold, address its source of growth, and get rid of the affected material.

Prevention: 

Steps are taken to prevent future mold growth, such as repairing leaks, improving ventilation, and maintaining proper humidity levels.

MOLD SANITIZATION:

Mold sanitization is a process of reducing the number of mold spores and bacteria to a safe and healthy level. Mold sanitization aims to sanitize the affected surfaces, reducing the number of harmful contaminants that can cause health problems.

The process of mold sanitization typically involves using specialized cleaning solutions and equipment to remove mold spores from affected surfaces. In some cases, a biocide or fungicide may also be used to kill the mold spores. However, it is important to note that the use of biocides or fungicides should be used with caution, as these chemicals can also be harmful to human health if not used carefully. Mold sanitization may be necessary after a mold remediation process has been completed to ensure that all mold spores have been eliminated from the affected area.
